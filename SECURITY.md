## Security Policy

This project takes security and reliability seriously. Although it is not a commercial product, any vulnerabilities or weaknesses can still impact users and the quality of the codebase.

If you discover a security issue, please follow the guidelines below.

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability (for example: data exposure, authentication issues, serious logic flaws, or other high‑impact problems), please **do not** open a public issue immediately.

Instead, contact the maintainer directly:

- **Email:** `dev.hadi.karimi@gmail.com`

Please include:

- A clear and concise description of the issue.  
- Steps to reproduce the vulnerability (if possible).  
- Any relevant logs, screenshots, or proof‑of‑concept code.  
- The potential impact as you understand it.

---

## What to Expect

When you report a security issue privately:

1. **Acknowledgment**  
   - You should receive an acknowledgment of your report within a reasonable time.

2. **Investigation**  
   - The maintainer will review the report, attempt to reproduce the issue, and evaluate its severity.

3. **Fix and Coordination**  
   - If the issue is confirmed, a fix will be prepared.  
   - You may be contacted for additional details or to help verify the fix.

4. **Disclosure**  
   - The maintainer may document the fix in the changelog or release notes.  
   - Details of the vulnerability may be summarized in a way that does not expose sensitive information.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- The code in this repository and its configuration.  
- Authentication, authorization, and data handling flows implemented by the project.  
- Any deployed instance that is clearly identified as using this codebase in its original form.

Out of scope:

- Third‑party services not controlled by the maintainer.  
- Issues caused purely by development or misconfiguration in forks or unrelated deployments.

---

## Responsible, Professional Disclosure

Please act responsibly when investigating and reporting issues:

- Do not intentionally abuse, exfiltrate, or destroy data.  
- Do not perform denial‑of‑service or other destructive testing.  
- Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability before the maintainer has had a reasonable chance to address it.

Thank you for helping keep this project safe and professional.
